## Principles & Scope Boundaries

- **Zero affiliate commissions**: We take no referral fees or kickbacks from JLCPCB, LCSC, PCBWay, Seeed Fusion, inspection agencies (V-Trust, QIMA, Sofeast, HQTS), or factories.
- **Checklists, not legal or engineering sign-off**: These files are buyer preparation checklists compiled from public regulatory rules and manufacturing practice — not legal representation, immigration advice, or EMC lab certification. Always confirm binding contracts with PRC-licensed IP counsel and statutory compliance with an accredited test lab.
- **Contributions welcome**: Spot a KiCad plugin update, LCSC fee change, or Shenzhen port policy shift? Open a PR or Issue.
