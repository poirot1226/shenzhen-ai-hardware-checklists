# 02 · China NNN Agreement & Shenzhen Factory Vetting Checklist (Buyer-Side)

Use this checklist before sending CAD, Gerber, firmware, or BOM files — and before wiring any tooling or production deposit to a Shenzhen / Pearl River Delta supplier.

> **Full Reference Guides (15 Red-Flag Clause Rewrites + 7-Step GSXT SOP):**

## Part 1 · Supplier Entity & Factory Verification (`gsxt.gov.cn`)

- [ ] **Collect the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (统一社会信用代码)**: Obtain an uncropped photo of the Chinese Business License before quoting or sharing IP.
- [ ] **Verify Active status on China SAMR (`gsxt.gov.cn`)**: Confirm status is `存续 / 在营` (Active), operating history (prefer ≥3 years), and zero unresolved `经营异常` (abnormal operation) flags.
- [ ] **Run Business Scope (`经营范围`) as a Step 1 exclusion filter only**: Reject pure `销售 / 批发 / 贸易` (trade-only) scopes if you need a direct factory — **but never treat `生产 / 制造` on a license as standalone proof of owned SMT lines or capacity** (licensed entities can still subcontract 100%).
- [ ] **Cross-check physical address type**: Manufacturing plants generally sit in industrial parks (`工业园 / 科技园`) in Bao'an, Longhua, Guangming, Longgang, or Dongguan; downtown commercial office towers (`大厦 / 广场`) in Futian/Luohu typically indicate trading offices or showrooms.
- [ ] **Enforce 3-Way Entity Triangulation (zero mismatches)**: Confirm that **(A)** the GSXT registered Chinese legal name, **(B)** the red Company Chop (`公章`) stamped on your contract/PO, and **(C)** the corporate bank beneficiary receiving your T/T deposit all match one identical legal entity. Never wire B2B deposits to personal accounts.
- [ ] **Conduct an unscheduled 15-minute live video walk-through**: Start outside the exterior gate signage and walk uninterrupted onto active SMT/assembly lines, QC racks, and warehouse.
- [ ] **Book an independent Pre-Shipment Inspection (PSI) before final 70% balance**: For commercial runs (typically >$3,000–$5,000), use an independent zero-kickback third-party inspector (e.g., V-Trust, QIMA, Sofeast, or HQTS) to catch silent BOM substitutions before cargo ships.

## Part 2 · Buyer-Side China NNN & Tooling Red-Flag Screen

- [ ] **Cover all three N's (Non-Disclosure, Non-Use, Non-Circumvention)**: Standard Western NDAs focus on third-party leaks while leaving factory self-manufacturing and direct customer solicitation uncovered.
- [ ] **Require bilingual EN/CN text where Chinese prevails**: PRC courts and CIETAC tribunals operate in Chinese; English-only contracts invite costly translation delays.
- [ ] **Specify enforceable PRC governing law + dispute forum**: Your forum clause (PRC court where supplier assets sit, or CIETAC arbitration) determines whether a judgment is practically collectible.
- [ ] **Require the official round red Company Chop (`公章`)**: A sales rep's English signature alone rarely binds a PRC corporate entity.
- [ ] **Include fixed liquidated damages (`违约金`)**: PRC courts favor pre-agreed liquidated damages over speculative lost Western profit calculations.
- [ ] **Bind sister shells & subcontractors explicitly**: Require prior written buyer consent before outsourcing PCBA/molding to neighbor workshops, with joint liability.
- [ ] **Lock physical Injection Mold / Tooling Title separately**: An NNN protects information, not steel. State 100% buyer title upon payment, engraved asset serials, zero third-party runs, and unconditional removal right — remembering that **mold custody and milestone payment control** are your strongest commercial leverage.
