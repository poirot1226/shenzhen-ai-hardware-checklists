# 03 · Shenzhen Entry, Foreign Payments & Huaqiangbei Field Checklist (2026–2027)

Practical pre-departure checklist for overseas hardware founders visiting Shenzhen for component sourcing, Huaqiangbei market scouting, or Guangdong factory audits.

> **Full 2026–2027 Reference Matrices (Official Sources & Port Rules):**
> - 3D Shenzhen Visa & Foreign Payment Matrix: https://shenzhenaihardware.com/shenzhen-visa-payment-guide

---

## A. Entry Path & Border Port Verification

- [ ] **Check 30-day visa-free eligibility by passport**: Ordinary passport holders from eligible countries (including UK & Canada added Feb 17, 2026, EU/Schengen, Australia, NZ, Japan, South Korea, plus Singapore mutual visa-free) can enter via any open Shenzhen port.
- [ ] **US passport holders — confirm non-30-day entry path**: US citizens are *not* on the 30-day unilateral visa-free list; plan either a pre-arranged M/L visa, a **240-hour visa-free transit** (`Country A → Shenzhen → Country C` confirmed onward ticket), or the **5-Day Shenzhen SEZ Port VOA** at eligible ports (Luohu / Huanggang / Shekou).
- [ ] **Respect the Dongguan / Guangzhou red line**: The 5-Day Shenzhen SEZ Port VOA legally restricts travel strictly inside Shenzhen city limits — do **not** cross into Dongguan or Guangzhou for SMT/molding plant visits on a Shenzhen Port VOA.
- [ ] **Match visa type to actual business activity**: Tourist (L) visas, 5-day Port VOA, and transit waivers do not authorize formal commercial operations; arrange an M business visa for multi-city factory audits.

## B. Foreign Payments & Connectivity Setup (Before Boarding)

- [ ] **Verify Alipay & WeChat Pay with foreign Visa/Mastercard BEFORE landing**: Complete passport real-name verification while you still have reliable SMS/email OTP access.
- [ ] **Know the 200 RMB fee threshold**: Foreign cards linked to Alipay/WeChat typically carry **0% fee on single transactions ≤200 RMB** and **3% fee above 200 RMB** (PBOC guidance).
- [ ] **Carry CNY 1,000–2,000 backup cash**: Physical Visa/Mastercard swipe terminals are rare in Huaqiangbei component stalls.
- [ ] **Install offline maps & roaming eSIM**: Set up Amap (高德地图) or Apple Maps + an international roaming eSIM so Gmail, Slack, and Google Docs remain reachable without disruption.

## C. Huaqiangbei District Routing & Buyer Reality

- [ ] **Arrive after 10:00 AM**: Major Huaqiangbei markets open around 9:30–10:00 AM and close around 6:30–7:00 PM.
- [ ] **Skip Mingtong Digital City for electronics**: Outdated blogs still list Mingtong for electronics, but Mingtong pivoted primarily to parallel-import cosmetics.
- [ ] **Target the right building by supply role**:
  - **SEG Plaza (赛格广场)** & **Huaqiang Electronic World (华强电子世界)** — ICs, passives, modules, dev boards, PC hardware & repair tools.
  - **Yuanwang Digital Mall (远望数码城)** & **Feiyang Times (飞扬时代)** — Smartphones, accessories & wholesale smart gadgets.
- [ ] **Separate showrooms from factories**: Treat Huaqiangbei booths as component bazaars and sales showrooms — real SMT, injection-molding, and OEM assembly plants operate 40–90 minutes away in Bao'an, Longhua, and Dongguan. Verify any OEM vendor via [Checklist 02](./02-china-nnn-and-factory-vetting.md) before wiring deposits.
